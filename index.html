Survive the month: 6 habits

Small changes that move your zero date later.

Pay bills on day one

Move rent, recharge and subscriptions out first so the rest of your money is clearly spendable.

Set a daily cap

Divide your spendable balance by the days left. That number is your safe daily budget.

Cap the weekend

Weekends cost more than weekdays. Pick one outing a weekend and set its limit in advance.

Share subscriptions

Split streaming and music plans with friends. Cancel anything you opened last month.

Walk or share rides

Short auto and cab trips add up quickly. Pool rides or take the bus where you can.

Keep a buffer

Park a small amount aside for emergencies so one surprise doesn't wreck the whole month.

Questions you might have

Quick answers about how the forecast works.

How is my daily spending calculated?

We take five weekdays and two weekend days, add them up and divide by seven. That blended daily burn rate is used for the forecast.

How is the safe daily budget worked out?

It is your spendable balance (allowance minus bills) divided by the number of days until your next allowance on the 1st.

What does the risk score mean?

It estimates how likely you are to overspend before the next credit, based on how fast you spend compared to what you have left.

Where do my inputs go?

Your numbers are sent to the BrokeMeter server to generate the prediction. The server may take a few seconds to wake up if it has been idle.
